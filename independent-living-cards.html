What will I receive?

One PNG ZIP, one JPG ZIP, a two-page A4 PDF, a two-page US Letter PDF and a three-page customer guide. The formats are alternative versions of the same 12 cards, not extra cards. These are finished files, not editable templates.

How do I print the cards?

Choose the PDF for your paper size and print at Actual Size / 100%. To print individual images at 5 × 5 cm, set those dimensions in your layout. Print a test, cut and optionally laminate with a sealed border.

How can I use the prompts?

Choose a short visual strip, portable set or communication board. Allow processing time and respect individual choices. The Medication card does not specify a medicine, dose or schedule; use it only alongside existing instructions and agreed support.

How may I use the files?

For personal or classroom use only. Redistribution or resale is not permitted. Contact the studio about wider use.
