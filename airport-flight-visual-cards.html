How do I print and use them?

Print on A4 at 100% / Actual Size, then cut around each card. Select the steps you need and arrange them in your preferred order. Laminate if you prefer reusable cards.

Useful information

Always follow airport security and airline instructions. Quieter queues and assistance vary by airport; these cards help you ask and do not guarantee access.
