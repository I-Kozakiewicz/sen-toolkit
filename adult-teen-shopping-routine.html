SUPPORT FOR EVERY STAGE

Plan the trip. Follow the steps.

Choose the cards that fit the person, the shop and the support they prefer.

BEFORE YOU GO

Feel prepared

Check what is needed, make a list and remember the essentials: payment, bags, phone and belongings. Use the travel prompts that suit the journey.

AT THE SHOP

Make the next step clear

Find an aisle, check an item and its price, choose food and cross it off the list. Keep help and quieter-space prompts within reach.

CHECKOUT & HOME

Finish the routine

Follow the queue, scanning, payment and packing steps, then travel home, bring the shopping inside and put it away.

What is included?

45 Shopping Routine visual cards as individual PNG images in a ZIP file, plus a printable PDF. The PDF and PNGs contain the same card set, so you can choose how to use it. These are finished resources, not editable templates.

Who can use this resource?

Adults and teenagers who find visual prompts useful, including neurodivergent people. Use it for personal routines, preparing a trip with family or a support worker, supported living, or practising community and independent living skills.

How can I use the cards?

Start with the steps that are useful to the person. Arrange a short sequence before a trip, refer to individual prompts while shopping, or use the PNG files on a device. Print the PDF at 100% / Actual Size and cut out the cards; laminate if you prefer. Adapt the sequence to the person’s choices and support needs.

Is this a physical product?

No. This is a digital download for you to print or use digitally. No printed cards or accessories will be posted. Purchase through our Payhip checkout to access your digital files.
