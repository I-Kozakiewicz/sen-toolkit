ABOUT THE STUDIO

Support should feel
like it belongs to you.

Communication is a fundamental right. Growing up should never mean growing out of access to visual support.

At The SEN & Co Toolkit Studio, we design with teens and adults in mind: everyday language, considered visuals and a calm, age-appropriate feel.

Whether you are choosing something for yourself or supporting someone else, the starting point is the person: their preferences, their routines and their way of communicating.

A FEW USEFUL ANSWERS

Before you choose.

Who are these resources for?

Our focus is neurodivergent teens and adults, including people choosing their own supports and those helping someone find resources that suit them. Individual preferences and access needs come first.

Where do I buy and download resources?

Open a featured product and choose Buy now to continue to Payhip. After purchase, your files are available to download and a download link is emailed to you. Other resources are available in our Etsy shop.

Will I receive a physical item?

The ready-made range is digital. Read the product description before buying to check the files supplied, printing requirements and permitted use.

Can I request different wording or visuals?

Yes. Tell us what you need through the custom-request area below. We can discuss tailored communication supports, routine systems and custom illustrations, and confirm what is possible before work begins.

How do I choose the right support?

Consider the person's own preferences, familiar words and images, and the situation where the resource will be used. Review the listing examples carefully and contact the studio if you need more detail.
