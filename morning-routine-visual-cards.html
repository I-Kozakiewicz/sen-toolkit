What will I receive?

A digital download containing 50 Morning Routine Visual Cards for Teens & Adults as a printable PDF and individual PNGs, with routine and printing guidance. The lifestyle photographs show examples of how cards can be arranged; props are not included.

How do I print the cards?

Print the PDF at Actual Size / 100% rather than Fit to Page. For individual PNGs, set each card to 5 × 5 cm in your print layout. Print a test page and check the size before printing the complete set. Cut the cards and optionally laminate them, leaving a sealed margin.

How do I build a morning routine?

Choose relevant cards, arrange them in a familiar order and start with a manageable number of steps. Place the sequence somewhere easy to see and adapt it to the individual’s preferences and pace.

Is this a physical product?

No. This is a printable digital download. You provide your own printing materials and any board, clips or other accessories.
