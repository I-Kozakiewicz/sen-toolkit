What activities does the pack cover?

Household tasks, preparing to go out, transport, shopping, work and study, personal care, sensory needs and asking for support. Examples include packing a backpack, laundry, checking a schedule, waiting in line, asking for help and taking a break.

What will I receive?

A4 Parts 1 and 2 and US Letter Parts 1 and 2, each with two pages, plus a three-page customer guide. Choose one paper size and print both parts for all 45 cards. These are finished printable PDFs, not editable templates. Individual image files are not included.

How do I print the cards?

Choose the PDF for your paper size and print at Actual Size / 100%. Print both parts in your chosen paper size for the full set. Print a test, cut and optionally laminate with a sealed border.

How can I use the prompts?

Choose a short visual strip, portable set or communication board. Allow processing time and respect individual choices. The Medication card does not specify a medicine, dose or schedule; use it only alongside existing instructions and agreed support.

How may I use the files?

For personal or classroom use only. Redistribution or resale is not permitted. Contact the studio about wider use.
