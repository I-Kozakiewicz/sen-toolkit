What will I receive?

One ZIP with 11 individual PNG cards, one A4 printable PDF with all 11 labelled cards at 5 × 5 cm, and a three-page welcome and usage guide. These are finished images and PDFs, not editable templates.

How do I print the cards?

Print the A4 sheet at Actual Size / 100%. Cut and optionally laminate with a sealed border. Individual PNGs can also be arranged into a personalised layout.

Who is this pack for?

Teens and adults who benefit from visual reminders for an established shaving routine. The images show a manual razor. Follow the equipment instructions and provide appropriate instruction or supervision where needed; the cards are not a complete lesson in razor technique.
