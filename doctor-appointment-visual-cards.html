How do I print at 5 × 5 cm?

Print the PDF on A4 paper at 100% / Actual size. Turn off Fit to page and Shrink to fit. Check a card with a ruler before cutting along its outer edges. Captions are small at this compact size.

Useful information

These cards are visual communication prompts, not medical advice. Choose the optional cards that help you; they do not need to be used as a sequence.

The artwork was generated with AI and depicts fictional adults. This download does not automatically unlock a purchase inside the NEXT app.
