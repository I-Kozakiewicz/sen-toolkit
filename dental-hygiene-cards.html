A ROUTINE THAT FITS

Prepare. Brush. Communicate.

Choose the steps that suit the person. Use a few cards or arrange a complete routine.

PREPARE

Start with the essentials

Get the toothbrush and toothpaste ready, with separate prompts for opening, applying and closing the toothpaste.

BRUSH

Follow clear steps

Visual prompts for brushing different tooth surfaces, keeping going, spitting out toothpaste and putting things away.

COMMUNICATE

Express a need

Cards for asking for help, communicating tooth or gum discomfort, requesting supplies and asking to visit the dentist.

What will I receive?

Three ZIP files containing all 30 cards: Part 1 has cards 1–10, Part 2 has cards 22–30, and Part 3 has cards 11–21. Also included: a three-page printable PDF and a four-page buyer guide. The files are finished PNG images and PDFs, not editable Canva templates.

How do I print the cards?

Print the A4 PDF at Actual Size / 100%. For individual PNGs, set each card to 5 × 5 cm in your print layout. Print a test page, cut and optionally laminate with a sealed margin.

Who is this pack for?

Teens and adults who benefit from visual prompts for toothbrushing and communicating dental-care needs. Review the preview to decide whether the imagery and wording fit the intended user. Allow processing time and adapt the sequence to their preferences.

How may I use the files?

For personal use by the purchaser with one household or one individual client. You may print replacement copies for that permitted use. Do not share, resell, upload or redistribute the files or artwork. Contact the studio for wider professional or multi-user licensing.
