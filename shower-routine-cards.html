A ROUTINE THAT FITS

Prepare. Wash. Get ready.

Choose the steps that suit the person. Use a few cards or arrange a complete routine.

PREPARE

Start with the essentials

Gather clothes, a towel and shower products, turn on the shower and check the water temperature.

WASH

Follow clear steps

Separate prompts for shampoo, conditioner, washing different areas and rinsing.

FINISH

Move on with your day

Turn off the shower, step out, dry, dress, hang up the towel and put dirty clothes in the laundry.

What will I receive?

Three ZIP files containing cards 01–12, 13–24 and 25–34; a three-page A4 PDF with all 34 cards; and a three-page buyer guide. The files are finished PNG images and PDFs, not editable Canva templates.

How do I print the cards?

Print the A4 PDF at Actual Size / 100%. For individual PNGs, set each card to 5 × 5 cm in your print layout. Print a test page, cut and optionally laminate with a sealed margin.

Who is this pack for?

Teens and adults who benefit from visual prompts for showering and personal-care routines. Review the preview to decide whether the imagery and wording fit the intended user. Allow processing time and adapt the sequence to their preferences.

How may I use the files?

For the purchaser within one household, classroom or direct professional caseload. Print copies for your own authorised use. Digital files must not be shared, resold, uploaded, copied for colleagues or incorporated into another product for sale.
