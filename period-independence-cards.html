What will I receive?

Two ZIP files and one three-page PDF guide. The ZIPs contain PNG and JPG versions of the same 38 cards, not 76 different cards. The guide covers downloading, printing and using the cards. A ready-arranged printable sheet PDF is not included.

How do I print the cards?

Extract a ZIP and place your chosen images into a document or print layout. Set each card to 5 × 5 cm, keep its square proportions and print at Actual Size / 100%. Check a test print before printing more. Cut and optionally laminate with a sealed border.

Who is this pack for?

Teens and adults who benefit from visual prompts for period care and communicating needs. Choose steps that match the person’s products, preferences and routine. The cards support communication and do not replace individual health advice.

Questions about using the files?

These are finished images, not editable templates. For questions about permitted use or wider sharing, email the studio.
