Routine support and safety

Next helps organise everyday steps using optional timers, pictures and progress records. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical condition. Consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. A check or photo records your observation; it does not detect whether an appliance is off or safe, or automatically analyse an image. Check the appliance yourself. Timers do not turn equipment off or complete a step. Notifications and sync may be delayed or missed. Do not rely on this app for emergencies, urgent care, medication safety or continuous supervision. Recording Help needed saves a choice; it does not itself call or notify anyone. The separate optional Notify my support person action requires sharing and explicit confirmation. Sending does not confirm delivery, reading or a response. Contact a person directly if you need help.

When subscription access expires, paid library images lock. Your saved routine, titles, notes, timers, own photos and history are not deleted because the subscription ended.
