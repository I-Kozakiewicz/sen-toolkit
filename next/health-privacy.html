Consumer health data privacy

Your choices

Routine and step names, notes, progress and requests for help may reveal health information if you enter details about medication, disability or health appointments. You choose what to enter. With cloud saving enabled, NEXT uses Firebase to store this information for your routines and history. Step photos and videos stay on your phone.

Who can see it

If you share a routine, the account you choose can read its text, progress and recorded attempts. A support request sends a generic notification; the recipient can open NEXT to see the relevant routine and step while access is active. You can stop sharing in My Support Circle.

Requests and appeals

To ask what information we hold or whom we shared it with, request a copy, correction or deletion, or appeal a decision, contact the Studio at the email below. Tell us which request you are asking about. Please leave out diagnoses and photos. You can also withdraw cloud consent in Settings.

Where the information comes from

You supply routine names, steps and notes. When you mark a step or ask for help, NEXT records that action. A link to a shared routine comes from its owner using your support code.

Changing your mind

In My Support Circle, find the person and routine under Access I have granted and choose Stop sharing. To request deletion of cloud account data, choose Request account deletion in Settings. To appeal our response, reply to the Studio and tell us which decision you want reviewed.

If your appeal is declined

We will explain our decision. If you are a Washington consumer, you can also file a complaint with the Washington State Attorney General.

Service providers and sharing

Google Firebase processes cloud records linked to your account. If you email the Studio, Microsoft handles your email address and the message you choose to send. The Studio uses correspondence to answer requests. We do not sell consumer health data or use it for advertising. The Studio has no affiliates receiving these data. Your chosen support person can see shared steps, progress and recorded attempts until you revoke access.
