Who is responsible

Accounts and cloud routines

Firebase Authentication handles your email, password authentication and account identifier. It also processes IP addresses and device/SDK information for service operation and abuse prevention. We do not store your password in routine documents or give support staff access to a plaintext password. Creating an account transmits authentication information even if you later choose local routine storage. If you choose cloud saving, Cloud Firestore stores routine titles, descriptions, steps, timer choices, colours, opaque media keys, update times, sharing permissions, current progress, attempt times and Done/Skip/Help needed results, linked to your account. These records provide routine saving, history and optional sharing.

Optional Google sign-in

If you choose Google sign-in, Google and Firebase Authentication process your Google account identifier, email address and available basic profile information such as your display name and profile photo URL to authenticate you. Next does not request access to your Google Drive, contacts or calendar. Connecting Google from Settings adds a sign-in method to your existing Next account and keeps its account identifier. It does not back up photos or videos. Your existing password sign-in remains available.

Photos, videos and local mode

Step photos and videos, check photos and their save-time/step metadata stay in account-scoped app storage on this phone. Their bytes are not uploaded to Firebase or sent to support people in this version. Opaque references to step media are saved with cloud routines. Your camera or gallery may keep separate originals. From version 0.5.14, local mode keeps you signed in and separates routines, history and media by account on this phone. Older versions used a shared local workspace. Recovering those records requires confirming ownership and the target account; recovery is then reserved for that account. Original legacy records and media remain on this device until app storage is cleared. Recovery copies available media and reports missing files. You may choose to copy cloud routine definitions to local storage before withdrawing cloud consent; this does not copy cloud history or media from other phones. Local routine content is not automatically uploaded or shared. Choosing local storage does not delete your cloud account. An optional greeting name is stored for your account on this phone, without uploading it. Language, appearance, sound, vibration, reminders and privacy choices are also local. Your phone time zone is used for local reminders.

Optional sensitive information and your choice

We do not ask for a diagnosis. Routine text and progress may still reveal health or disability information. Cloud saving requires a separate, explicit choice explaining this processing; its checkbox starts unticked. You can instead use local routines on this phone. We use ordinary account/service data to provide the service you request; essential security and support rely on legitimate interests, subject to your rights, and legal obligations where applicable. For health or disability information in cloud routines and related progress, we rely on your explicit consent in addition to the applicable ordinary-data basis. Your consent is not permission for advertising, diagnosis or research. You can withdraw through Settings. Withdrawal requires a server-confirmed update. Once confirmed, access to cloud routines, progress and history is blocked for the account and supporters. Connected devices running this version close their private screens; previously loaded information on offline or older devices cannot be remotely erased. Withdrawal itself requests erasure of cloud routines, progress, history and sharing records; no separate email is needed. You may save routine definitions to this phone before withdrawal; cloud history and media from other phones are not copied. Without that copy, cloud-only routines will be lost. Cleanup is handled without undue delay through our withdrawal queue. Cloud saving stays blocked until cleanup is confirmed; fresh consent starts without the deleted content or old sharing. Your sign-in account, purchase entitlements and local photos remain. A minimal account privacy record stores your choice, policy revision, change number and timestamp. Sharing identifiers may remain while cleanup is pending, but do not permit access to routine content. The minimal privacy record also stores the completed cleanup generation to prevent stale work from deleting new data. It is kept while the account exists for enforcing privacy choices, not to retain routine content. Purchase cancellation, refunds and required security or legal records are handled separately. Withdrawal does not affect earlier lawful processing. Do not enter other people’s health information without an appropriate lawful basis.

Sharing and service providers

Other users cannot read your routines unless you explicitly share a selected routine. The chosen person can read its text, future changes, current progress and recorded attempts; they cannot edit it or see local photos/videos. Verify their support code and revoke access in Support Circle when needed. Revocation cannot recall copies already made. Google Firebase provides account/database services; Microsoft provides our support email service. Authorised service administrators may access cloud records when necessary for support, security, maintenance or legal duties. This is not end-to-end encryption. We do not sell data, use it for advertising or include Analytics, Crashlytics or Gemini in this version. Google Play and device services process installation/security information under their own terms. Email and tester feedback are used for support/testing only.

Camera and selected-media access are used only when you start adding media. The phone’s video camera may record sound. Optional notifications use generic text. Declining media or notifications does not prevent text routines. We do not request GPS location, contacts or Health Connect records. Local storage is not a guarantee of absolute security: protect your phone and review who can unlock it. No independent security certification has been completed.

Retention and deletion

Accounts and routine records remain while your account is active; history display limits are not deletion schedules. Deleting a routine does not automatically remove its previous attempts. Sign-out does not erase cloud records or local media. Request deletion in Settings or at thesenandcotoolkitstudio.com/next/delete-account.html, or email thesenandcotoolkitstudio@hotmail.com from your account address. We verify ownership proportionately without asking for your password. We manually delete the authentication account, cloud routines/history and all associated nested activity and sharing grants without undue delay, normally within one calendar month of receiving your request. If proportionate identity checks or a lawful extension affect the deadline, we will explain what is needed and why. We confirm the outcome when complete. We retain closed support/deletion correspondence for up to 12 months for request handling and accountability, then remove it unless a specific legal obligation requires longer. Any exception needs a recorded reason and end date. Firebase separately retains Authentication IP logs for a few weeks and removes other authentication data from live/backup systems within 180 days after customer-initiated deletion, according to its published guidance. Immediate erasure from every provider backup is not promised.

Clearing app data or uninstalling removes app-local copies, not cloud records or originals in your gallery. We cannot remotely erase media on another offline phone. Local photos do not automatically move to a new phone.

Deletion safeguard

After account deletion, we retain a server-only security marker derived from your account identifier to prevent delayed purchase messages from recreating deleted records. It contains no email, routine content, photos or purchase tokens. It is retained while needed to block that processing, and its necessity is reviewed when the purchase service is retired or changed.

Purchases and private delivery

For private purchase testing and enabled library purchases, Google Play handles checkout and payment details. Next does not receive your full bank-card number. We process your Next account identifier, product identifier, purchase token, purchase status, verification times and access expiry to verify, restore and protect purchases. Google receives a hashed Next account identifier for purchase association. Purchase tokens are kept in a server-only ledger; access records are account-scoped. Google Cloud Functions, Firestore, private Cloud Storage, Pub/Sub purchase notifications and Play Integrity/App Check support verification and private delivery. This processing provides the requested purchase service and protects it against misuse; it is separate from optional cloud-routine consent. Token and access records are retained while needed to verify or restore purchases; deleting the account is handled through the deletion procedure and does not cancel Google Play billing. Any legally required financial record retained separately must have a documented purpose and retention period. Google keeps its own payment records under its policies.

Optional read-aloud

If you enable read-aloud, Next passes the text to your phone's selected text-to-speech engine using an installed voice reported as not requiring a network connection. Next does not send this text to its own online voice service.

Optional private support notifications

This feature is restricted to two invited private-test accounts and is disabled in the wider tester build. When you enable it on a phone, we register its Firebase Cloud Messaging token, your account identifier, language, enablement and consent timestamps. An explicitly sent request stores sender and recipient account identifiers, routine and step identifiers, the sharing reference, request time, expiry and sending status. Google Firebase Cloud Messaging delivers generic notification text and an opaque request identifier, not routine text, photos or the person’s name. Opening it requires signed-in access and a fresh permission check before routine and step titles are displayed. Request access expires after five minutes; this is not a promise that every stored record or provider copy is deleted within five minutes. Disabling the feature through the app removes the server registration once confirmed. Blocking Android notifications alone does not necessarily remove that registration. Delivery and reading are not confirmed by a successful send. Sharing revocation blocks subsequent detail access but cannot recall a notification or copy already received. Existing cloud consent and sharing controls also apply. Registrations expire 30 days after the last device registration or token refresh. Registering again renews this period. Expired registrations cannot be used and are removed by scheduled cleanup; removal may be delayed if cleanup fails. Expired request records are handled by scheduled cleanup, separately from the five-minute access limit. Provider-held installation data has a separate lifecycle; removing our registration or a messaging token does not promise immediate removal of every provider copy. Optional server registration for support notifications is separate from technical processing by Firebase Authentication, App Check, Cloud Functions and their dependencies. These services may process account identifiers, IP addresses and installation or device information to operate and protect the service even when support notifications are not enabled. Turning off support notifications does not turn off account or security processing.

Comfort and language settings

Breathing guidance and optional rain are played within the app. The keep-screen-awake preference controls the display during active routines; it does not prevent manual locking. Library language selection changes the catalogue view, not saved routine content. These options do not share routine content with another person.

Google Firebase Cloud Messaging dostarcza ogólną treść powiadomienia i nieprzejrzysty identyfikator prośby, bez tekstu rutyny, zdjęć ani imienia osoby. Otwarcie wymaga zalogowania i ponownego sprawdzenia dostępu przed wyświetleniem tytułu rutyny i kroku. Dostęp do prośby wygasa po pięciu minutach; nie oznacza to gwarancji usunięcia każdego zapisu lub kopii usługodawcy w ciągu pięciu minut. Wyłączenie funkcji w aplikacji usuwa rejestrację na serwerze po potwierdzeniu. Sama blokada powiadomień Androida nie musi usuwać tej rejestracji. Udane wysłanie nie potwierdza dostarczenia ani odczytania. Cofnięcie udostępnienia blokuje późniejszy dostęp do szczegółów, ale nie wycofa otrzymanego powiadomienia ani kopii. Obowiązują również istniejące zasady zgody na chmurę i udostępniania. Rejestracja wygasa 30 dni po ostatniej rejestracji urządzenia lub odświeżeniu tokenu. Ponowna rejestracja odnawia ten okres. Wygasła rejestracja nie pozwala na używanie powiadomień i jest usuwana przez zaplanowane czyszczenie; awaria może opóźnić usunięcie. Wygasłe prośby obsługuje zaplanowane usuwanie, osobne od pięciominutowego limitu dostępu. Dane instalacji u usługodawcy mają osobny cykl przechowywania; usunięcie naszej rejestracji lub tokenu wiadomości nie oznacza natychmiastowego usunięcia wszystkich kopii usługodawcy. Opcjonalna rejestracja powiadomień wsparcia na serwerze jest odrębna od technicznego przetwarzania przez Firebase Authentication, App Check, Cloud Functions i ich zależności. Usługi te mogą przetwarzać identyfikatory konta, adres IP oraz informacje o instalacji lub urządzeniu w celu działania i ochrony usługi, również gdy powiadomienia wsparcia nie są włączone. Wyłączenie powiadomień wsparcia nie wyłącza przetwarzania danych konta ani zabezpieczeń.

Ustawienia komfortu i języka

Ćwiczenie oddechowe i opcjonalny deszcz działają w aplikacji. Ustawienie utrzymywania włączonego ekranu dotyczy aktywnych rutyn i nie blokuje ręcznego zablokowania telefonu. Wybór języka biblioteki zmienia widok katalogu, nie treść zapisanych rutyn. Te opcje nie udostępniają treści rutyn innej osobie.
