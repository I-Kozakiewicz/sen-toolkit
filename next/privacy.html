Optional Google sign-in

If you choose Google sign-in, Google and Firebase Authentication process your Google account identifier, email address and available basic profile information such as your display name and profile photo URL to authenticate you. Next does not request access to your Google Drive, contacts or calendar. Connecting Google from Settings adds a sign-in method to your existing Next account and keeps its account identifier. It does not back up photos or videos. Your existing password sign-in remains available.

Who is responsible

SEN & Co — Next is operated by Iwona Kozakiewicz, trading as The SEN & Co Toolkit Studio, United Kingdom. Iwona Kozakiewicz is the data controller. Contact us for privacy, support, access or deletion requests: thesenandcotoolkitstudio@hotmail.com.

Accounts and cloud routines

Firebase Authentication handles your email, password authentication and account identifier. It also processes IP addresses and device/SDK information for service operation and abuse prevention. We do not store your password in routine documents or give support staff access to a plaintext password. Creating an account transmits authentication information even if you later choose local routine storage. If you choose cloud saving, Cloud Firestore stores routine titles, descriptions, steps, timer choices, colours, opaque media keys, update times, sharing permissions, current progress, attempt times and Done/Skip/Help needed results, linked to your account. These records provide routine saving, history and optional sharing.

Photos, videos and local mode

Step photos and videos, check photos and their save-time/step metadata stay in account-scoped app storage on this phone. Their bytes are not uploaded to Firebase or sent to support people in this version. Opaque references to step media are saved with cloud routines. Your camera or gallery may keep separate originals. Local mode saves its own routines and history on this phone without uploading routine content or offering cloud sharing; it does not delete an account you previously created. Language, appearance, sound, vibration, reminders and privacy choices are also local. Your phone time zone is used for local reminders.

Optional sensitive information and your choice

We do not ask for a diagnosis. Routine text and progress may still reveal health or disability information. Cloud saving requires a separate, explicit choice explaining this processing; its checkbox starts unticked. You can instead use local routines on this phone. We use ordinary account/service data to provide the service you request; essential security and support rely on legitimate interests, subject to your rights, and legal obligations where applicable. For health or disability information in cloud routines and related progress, we rely on your explicit consent in addition to the applicable ordinary-data basis. Your consent is not permission for advertising, diagnosis or research. You can withdraw through Settings. This signs out and removes the cloud permission on this phone, then shows how to send a withdrawal/deletion request to us. Send that request so we can stop the relevant processing and erase cloud records; local withdrawal alone cannot notify us or stop another already signed-in phone. Withdrawal does not affect earlier lawful processing. Do not enter other people’s health information without an appropriate lawful basis.

Sharing and service providers

Other users cannot read your routines unless you explicitly share a selected routine. The chosen person can read its text, future changes, current progress and recorded attempts; they cannot edit it or see local photos/videos. Verify their support code and revoke access in Support Circle when needed. Revocation cannot recall copies already made. Google Firebase provides account/database services; Microsoft provides our support email service. Authorised service administrators may access cloud records when necessary for support, security, maintenance or legal duties. This is not end-to-end encryption. We do not sell data, use it for advertising or include Analytics, Crashlytics or Gemini in this version. Google Play and device services process installation/security information under their own terms. Email and tester feedback are used for support/testing only.

Permissions and security

Camera and selected-media access are used only when you start adding media. The phone’s video camera may record sound. Optional notifications use generic text. Declining media or notifications does not prevent text routines. We do not request GPS location, contacts or Health Connect records. Firebase connections use encryption in transit and account-based access rules. Persistent Firestore disk caching is disabled and Android app backups are excluded. Local storage is not a guarantee of absolute security: protect your phone and review who can unlock it. No independent security certification has been completed.

Storage and international transfers

The service uses Firebase and Microsoft email, whose providers and subprocessors may process data outside the UK or your country. A regional database does not mean all authentication, technical and email data stays in that region. Their applicable service/data-processing terms and transfer safeguards govern provider processing. Contact us for details of the applicable safeguards. We do not claim UK-only storage. See firebase.google.com/support/privacy for Firebase service information.

Retention and deletion

Accounts and routine records remain while your account is active; history display limits are not deletion schedules. Deleting a routine does not automatically remove its previous attempts. Sign-out does not erase cloud records or local media. Request deletion in Settings or at thesenandcotoolkitstudio.com/next/delete-account.html, or email thesenandcotoolkitstudio@hotmail.com from your account address. We verify ownership proportionately without asking for your password. We manually delete the authentication account, cloud routines/history and all associated nested activity and sharing grants within 30 days of a verified request. We retain closed support/deletion correspondence for up to 12 months for request handling and accountability, then remove it unless a specific legal obligation requires longer. Any exception needs a recorded reason and end date. Firebase separately retains Authentication IP logs for a few weeks and removes other authentication data from live/backup systems within 180 days after customer-initiated deletion, according to its published guidance. Immediate erasure from every provider backup is not promised.

Local media and other phones

Individual check photos can be deleted in History. Some replaced/removed step files may remain in private app storage. Settings provides a way to clear this account’s local media and reminder preferences on this phone. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes app-local copies, not cloud records or originals in your gallery. We cannot remotely erase media on another offline phone. Local photos do not automatically move to a new phone.

Konta i rutyny w chmurze

Firebase Authentication obsługuje adres e-mail, uwierzytelnianie hasłem i identyfikator konta. Przetwarza też adres IP i informacje o urządzeniu oraz oprogramowaniu, aby usługa działała i zapobiegała nadużyciom. Nie zapisujemy hasła w rutynach ani nie udostępniamy pomocy hasła w postaci jawnej. Utworzenie konta przesyła dane uwierzytelniania, nawet jeśli później wybierzesz lokalny zapis rutyn. Po wyborze chmury Cloud Firestore przechowuje tytuły, opisy i kroki rutyn, ustawienia timerów, kolory, klucze plików, daty zmian, uprawnienia, bieżący postęp, czas prób i wyniki: wykonano, pominięto lub potrzebna pomoc. Dane są powiązane z kontem i służą zapisowi rutyn, historii oraz opcjonalnemu udostępnianiu.

Zdjęcia, filmy i tryb lokalny

Zdjęcia i filmy kroków oraz zdjęcia sprawdzeń z opisem kroku i datą zapisu pozostają w pamięci aplikacji na tym telefonie, oddzielnie dla każdego konta. Ta wersja nie przesyła plików do Firebase ani do osób wspierających. Odwołania do plików kroków są zapisywane z rutyną w chmurze. Aparat lub galeria mogą mieć osobny oryginał. Tryb lokalny zapisuje własne rutyny i historię na telefonie bez przesyłania treści rutyn ani udostępniania przez chmurę; nie usuwa wcześniej utworzonego konta. Ustawienia języka, wyglądu, dźwięku, wibracji, przypomnień i prywatności są lokalne. Przypomnienia korzystają ze strefy czasowej telefonu.

Nie prosimy o diagnozę. Tekst rutyn i postęp mogą jednak ujawniać informacje o zdrowiu lub niepełnosprawności. Zapis w chmurze wymaga osobnego, wyraźnego wyboru z wyjaśnieniem; pole zgody jest początkowo odznaczone. Możesz zamiast tego wybrać lokalne rutyny. Zwykłe dane konta służą wykonaniu zamówionej usługi; niezbędne działania bezpieczeństwa i pomocy opieramy na uzasadnionym interesie z uwzględnieniem Twoich praw, a obowiązki prawne na przepisach. Dla zdrowotnych informacji w rutynach i postępie w chmurze opieramy się dodatkowo na wyraźnej zgodzie. Nie obejmuje ona reklam, diagnozy ani badań. Możesz ją wycofać w Ustawieniach. Telefon wyloguje Cię i usunie lokalny wybór chmury, następnie pokaże, jak wysłać wniosek o wycofanie zgody/usunięcie danych. Wyślij wniosek, abyśmy mogli zatrzymać odpowiednie przetwarzanie i usunąć dane w chmurze; sam lokalny wybór nie powiadamia nas i nie zatrzyma innego zalogowanego telefonu. Wycofanie nie zmienia zgodności wcześniejszego przetwarzania. Nie wpisuj cudzych danych zdrowotnych bez właściwej podstawy.
