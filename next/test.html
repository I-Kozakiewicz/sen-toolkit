What does testing involve?

  1. Request an invitation using the Google account email shown in your phone's Play Store.
  2. Once the closed test is approved and your email is added, we send you the joining link.
  3. Join, install from Google Play and try a simple everyday routine.
  4. Stay opted in for at least 14 continuous days and try the app regularly. Send honest feedback—positive or negative.

Android 7 or newer required. No payment or diagnosis is required. Participation is voluntary; you can leave at any time. This is an early testing version, so you may encounter bugs.

Already an internal tester? Tell us in your email so we can send the correct switching instructions. The earlier internal-test invitation is different.
